# Audi

Dark automotive marketing. The library's clearest example of **translucent
controls sitting directly on photography** — directly relevant to the glass rules
in `CLAUDE.md`.

**Study:** the full-screen chapter structure, pill-shaped translucent CTAs over
imagery, the paired primary/secondary button treatment, the footer model carousel
with explicit prev/next affordances, and the icon-plus-label quick-link row.

**Watch for:** how a frosted button stays legible over both a bright sky and a
dark car body in the same scroll — this is the worst-case-backdrop problem solved
in production; the consistent two-CTA rhythm at the end of every chapter; how the
back-to-top control stays available without competing for attention.

**Do not lift:** the four rings, Audi's typeface, vehicle and F1 photography, the
chapter layout as a whole.
