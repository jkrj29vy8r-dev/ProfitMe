# Dashboards

Data-dense product surfaces from anywhere — not tied to one brand.

**Study:** chart design, table density and readability, filter and date-range
patterns, KPI/stat tiles, sidebar and nav IA, drill-down flows, responsive
behavior when a wide table meets a narrow screen.

**Watch for:** what earns the top-left position; how many numbers appear before
the page stops being scannable; how comparison and trend are encoded without
clutter; how empty, loading, error and "not enough data yet" states are handled —
these are where most dashboards fall apart and where ProfitMe can win.

**Especially relevant to ProfitMe:** profit/revenue framing, positive vs. negative
value treatment, currency and percentage formatting, time-period comparison.

## Contents

**Verdikt** (`verdikt-*`) — dark analytics dashboard for an AI vehicle-intelligence
product, plus a capture of its landing and VIN-input flow. Four dashboard stills
covering desktop settled, desktop scrolled, desktop mid-load, and the stacked
mobile layout.

The mid-load capture is the most valuable one in the folder: it catches the
dashboard *before* its data animates in, which makes the entry choreography
readable when set against the settled frame.

> **Provenance: first-party, kept visually distinct** (confirmed 2026-08-01).
> Verdikt is the same owner's product but a separate one. Take its **structural**
> patterns freely — module cards, eyebrow labels, entry choreography, sample-data
> state, reflow behavior. Do **not** carry over its palette or type voice:
> ProfitMe gets its own, so the two never read as the same product. The
> originality rules apply to Verdikt's surface, not to its structure.
