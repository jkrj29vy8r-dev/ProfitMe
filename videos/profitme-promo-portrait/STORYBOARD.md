The portrait cut of the landscape film. Same story, same tokens, same timings —
but the layouts are recomposed rather than scaled: a row of four tiles becomes a
2×2 grid, a horizontal waterfall becomes a vertical descent, and a timeline with
a label column becomes stacked blocks each carrying their own track. Nothing is
dropped for the narrow frame; it reflows.

## Video direction

Identical to the landscape cut in every token and rule — ground, copper-only
accent, semantic colour never carried by hue alone, opacity-first reveals, 40ms
cascades, tabular figures, shell → labels → numbers → geometry, a held ending.

The portrait-specific rule: **reflow, don't shrink.** Type sizes stay within one
step of the landscape cut rather than being scaled down to fit; where a
horizontal arrangement will not survive 1080px, the arrangement changes. Every
element stays above the 1594px keep-out line so a platform caption bar cannot
cover it.
