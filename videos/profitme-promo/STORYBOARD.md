## Video direction

**Ground.** Warm paper `#FBFAF8` on every frame, never pure white. Copper
`#955823` is the only accent and appears only as chrome — eyebrows, numerals,
rules, the single CTA. Green and red appear exclusively inside the semantic
moments of frames 2, 3 and 5, always with a sign or a written label beside them.

**Motion.** Transform and opacity only. Reveals resolve in place rather than
sliding — the design system bans the slide-in as the generic move. Groups cascade
top-to-bottom or left-to-right at 40ms; a group never appears at once. Entering
uses `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`, leaving uses `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 1, 1)`;
nothing is symmetric, nothing springs or bounces.

**Data.** Bars grow from zero on mount, lines draw, figures count up once. A value
that changes afterwards tweens — it never re-grows. Every comparable figure is
tabular, so digits hold their column as they animate.

**Assembly order.** Within any data frame: shell → labels → headline numbers →
charts and rows last. The number a viewer came for arrives before its decoration.

**Endings.** Every frame holds for at least a beat after its last element lands,
and the film ends on stillness rather than on a cut.
